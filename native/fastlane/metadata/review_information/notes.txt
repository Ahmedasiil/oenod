OENOD is a workforce app for businesses: time clock, schedules, time off, pay stubs, team list and a business wallet. Company accounts are created on https://oenod.com; the app is where owners and employees use them every day.

Demo account: sign in with the email above. It is the owner of "OENOD Demo Cafe", a sample company with 5 staff and 2 weeks of shifts, so every screen has data. Tap "Sign in" on the first screen.

Location: used only while the app is open, to confirm the person is at their workplace when they clock in. If the employer turns on site attendance, location is shared with managers only while clocked in and the app is open. Version 3.7 does not use background location (UIBackgroundModes location removed).

Apple Watch (3.7.0 build 371, fixed): the watch now keeps its pairing code when watchOS closes and reopens the app (before, it made a new code, so the code allowed on the iPhone no longer matched), and the iPhone ignores old codes. Steps: 1) Sign in on the iPhone with the demo account. 2) Open OENOD on the Apple Watch and tap "Agree and connect". 3) The iPhone app opens "Connect a watch" with the code filled in; tap "Allow and connect". If it does not open by itself: iPhone > Me > Connect a watch, type the 6-digit code shown on the watch, tap "Allow and connect". 4) On the watch, tap "I tapped Allow" or just wait a few seconds: it shows the time clock.
Camera: used only to scan QR work badges.
Face ID: optional quick unlock.
Account deletion: Me tab, Delete my account (also at https://oenod.com/m/delete-account).
There are no purchases inside the app. Companies sign up and manage their plan on oenod.com.
