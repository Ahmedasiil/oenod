OENOD is the all-in-one app for businesses and the people who work there. One login for owners, managers and every employee.

FOR OWNERS AND MANAGERS
• Live home screen: today's sales, orders, labor and who is working right now
• Approve time off and shift swaps in one tap
• Weekly schedule with open shifts, requests and next or previous week
• Your whole team at a glance: working now, on break or on leave
• Business wallet: see your balance, get paid and transfer to your bank
• Notifications for everything that needs you

FOR EMPLOYEES
• Clock in and out with one tap, with breaks
• See your next shift and your full schedule
• Request vacation, sick days and personal time
• Pay stubs, year-to-date totals and direct deposit
• Your hours for the last 7 and 31 days
• A QR work badge and a digital business card to share

BUILT FOR TRUST
• Face ID to open the app quickly
• Bank-grade security and two-step sign-in
• Location is used only to confirm you are at work when you clock in, and never on breaks or after you clock out
• 10 languages: English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, Punjabi, Tagalog, Hindi, Portuguese and Urdu
• Light and dark appearance
• Delete your account any time from the Me tab
